# Short Read Track

The short read alignment track consists of two parts. The top is a coverage plot on a log2 scale for the read coverage. The second part only show when you are sufficiently zoomed in and shows the individual reads.\r\n\r\nThe color key for the default color scheme.


|Color|Description|
|---|---|
|Green|Read mapped to the forward strand|
|Blue|Read mapped to the reverse strand|
|Yellow|Mismatch between the read and the reference, the read nucleotide will be shown when zoomed in|
|Red|Gap/deletion in the read|
|Black|Insertion in the read. Hover over them to see inserted bases.|
|Gray|Insertion in the read that is a multiple of 3. Hover over them to see inserted bases.|
