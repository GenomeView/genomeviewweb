# Gene Structure
Consists of 9 lines

* Top 3 are the forward reading frames
* #4 is the forward sequence
* #5 is a rules
* #6 is the reverse sequence
* The bottom 3 are the reverse strand reading frames

### Coloring in the reading frames
By default the potential start codons are indicated in green, the stop codons in red.

The exons of a CDS will be indicated in the correct reading frame.

### Coloring in the sequence lines
By default the potential canonical splice sites are indicated in blue (acceptor) and yellow (donor).
