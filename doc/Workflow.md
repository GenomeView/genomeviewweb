
A [Genome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genome) is a sequence of [nucleotides](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nucleotide), commonly represented by letters like A,C,G,T. 
Analyzing a genome is also called sequencing. Complete sequences can be stored in [FASTA files](PrepareFASTA.md).

Short read sequencing is a method to analyze genomes. In this method, genomes are broken into small pieces, typically ranging from 50 to 600 base pairs, which are then sequenced simultaneously. To reconstruct the full genome from these small sequences, all pieces are matched and aligned with a known full sequence  of a reference genome that has been sequenced in its entirity.
There must be sufficient overlap in the available sequences to fill up the entire reference genome.
