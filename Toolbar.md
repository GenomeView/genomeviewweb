# Toolbar

<img src="/img/toolbar.png" />
<b>Toolbar at the top of the window</b>

The toolbar consists of a number of icons and a text field. 
From left to right:

|toolbar item|
|---|---|
|counterclockwise arrow|undo?|
|clockwise arrow|redo?|
|magnify +|zoom in|
|magnify -|zoom out|
|arrow left|scroll towards start of genome|
|arrow right|scroll towards end of genome|
|chromosome|Select currently visible chromosome|
